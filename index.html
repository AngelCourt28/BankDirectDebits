What is a Direct Debit?

A Direct Debit is an automated way for an organisation to collect payments directly from your bank account.

You give an organisation permission to request payments from your bank account through a Direct Debit Instruction.

If something goes wrong with the collection of your Direct Debit, you may be protected by the Direct Debit Guarantee.

How do Card Payments work?

When you pay by card, your card provides information about the purchase to the card reader or payment system.

The card network communicates with your bank and the business receiving the payment to process the transaction.

The transaction is then settled between the relevant banks, allowing the business to receive the money.
